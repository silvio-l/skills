# Phase C: Three concepts

Load this after the interview summary. Also load `creative-principles.md`, the occasion's section
in `occasions.md`, and `style-kit.md` for the looks.

## C1. Generate wide, then pick three

Sketch at least five directions privately first, each from a different **axis**:
- a different **share trigger** ("that's so us" vs. "simple solution");
- a different **hook mechanism** (curiosity gap, visual surprise, everyday relevance, surprising
  claim);
- a different **protagonist** (the user, the problem as a character, an object, the recurring
  element or mascot, the product);
- a different **dramaturgy** (escalation → helper → change; question → reveal; build-up →
  lockup; loop);
- a different **style family** (typographic, illustrative, 3D, retro, UI-led);
- a different **tone** (dry, absurd, warm, epic).

Then keep three that differ on **at least three of these axes each**, and that are all producible
with the confirmed assets, budget, and HyperFrames capabilities. At least one of them should be
the bolder option (less expected, higher ceiling, a named risk).

**Substantial difference check** (do it explicitly): if two concepts share the same hook mechanism
*and* the same protagonist *and* the same dramaturgy, replace one. Swapping only the style or the
colors does not make a different concept.

## C2. Each concept contains

Write each concept in the conversation language, with this structure:

```markdown
### Concept <A|B|C>: "<working title>"

**In one sentence:** <what happens and why it sticks>

**First seconds (0–3 s), concretely**
- Picture: <the exact first frame: what is where>
- Text: "<exact on-screen text, if any>"
- Motion: <what moves first, and how>
- Sound: <first sound: SFX, music hit, voice line, or deliberate silence>
- Hook mechanism: <curiosity gap | visual surprise | everyday relevance | surprising claim (evidence: …)>

**The story** (setup → development → climax → resolution → close), 4–7 short beats with rough
times, each beat saying what *acts* and what *reacts*.

**Open question that keeps people watching:** <the question the viewer wants answered, and when it is answered>

**Share trigger:** <"that's so us" | "simple solution" | other>: who sends it to whom, and why
**Rewatch trigger:** <punchline or staging detail that rewards a second view>

**Product / audience link:** <which verified fact or benefit it carries (evidence path); which
audience segment recognizes itself>
**Recognition:** <visual signature + recurring element (object, figure, sound motif) + brand
elements used (logo file, colors, fonts)>

**Style:** <dominant style (+ accent and the logic for combining them)>, adapted to the brand:
<1–2 concrete rules>
**Duration / format:** <s, aspect ratio>
**Effort:** <S | M | L>: what drives it
**Asset needs:** <exists: paths> / <to create: what, how> / <paid: none | what, estimated cost class>
**Main risks:** <for example: the joke may not land for segment X; 3D effort; a claim needs a source; reading time>
```

Rules:
- Use only verified facts and properly marked claims. Every surprising claim needs its evidence
  named here.
- Plan no fake UI, no roadmap features, no invented numbers, testimonials, or vote results.
- Respect the confirmed format, length, language, and budget. If a concept needs paid generation
  that has not been approved, say so in its asset needs and risks; don't hide it.

A short table, with words only and no scores:

| Resolution (does it pay off the hook? punchline strength?) | | | |

**No virality scores, no reach forecasts, no invented percentages.** Comparative language only
("stronger opening than B because …").

## C4. Recommend and ask

Recommend one concept with a 2–4 sentence reason tied to the goals (reach first, recall second,
or the user's stated goal) and the comparison. Mention what the recommendation trades away.
Then ask the reserved selection question (it counts toward the budget of 10):

```
Question <n>/10 — Which concept should I turn into the production prompt?
a) Concept <X> (recommended)
b) Concept <Y>
c) Concept <Z>
d) Mix: tell me which parts
Or answer freely.
```

Wait for the answer, unless the user delegated the choice (then state "I'm taking <X> because …"
and continue). A "mix" answer is folded in without another question. If the mix is incoherent,
say how you resolved it.

Save the concepts, the comparison, and the choice in `<plan-dir>/concepts.md`.
