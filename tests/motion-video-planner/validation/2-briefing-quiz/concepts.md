## Concept A: "Ist das wirklich schneller?"

**In one sentence:** A knowledge quiz with a held thinking time; the answer options react while the viewer decides, and a thermometer scale reveals the tiny real effect.

**First seconds (0–3 s), concretely**
- Picture: black-green frame (#1F3D2B), a code-drawn pot in mustard (#E8B931) centered in the lower half, a thermometer stripe on the left edge.
- Text: "Salz ins Nudelwasser:/Kocht es schneller?"
- Motion: the pot's lid rattles once (2 px, 0.2 s), then the text slams in (`expo.out`, 0.25 s).
- Sound: short tick SFX on the text slam.
- Hook mechanism: curiosity gap.

**The story** (4–6 beats)
1. 0–3 s — hook: question on screen; pot rattles.
2. 3–8 s — options appear as two mustard tiles: "Ja, schneller" / "Nein, gleich". Tiles wobble in turn (escalation of tension without a countdown).
3. 8–14 s — thinking time: the thermometer climbs from 20 to 100 °C; both tiles lean toward the thermometer; a deliberate stillness of 0.5 s at 100 °C.
4. 14–19 s — reveal: "Nein." The thermometer's top is zoomed: "100,0 °C" becomes "100,5 °C" at the scale of the source.
5. 19–23 s — why: "Salz hebt den Siedepunkt an: 0,5 °C bei 29,2 g pro kg Wasser" (quote from UIUC source). A bubble pops on the explanation.
6. 23–25 s — close: logo lockup 1080×1080 scaled to 640 px, ambient idle, sound motif.

**Open question that keeps people watching:** "Ja oder nein?" — answered at 14 s.

**Share trigger:** "simple solution" — someone who salts early forwards it to the friend who does the same, as a quick fact.
**Rewatch trigger:** the 0.5 °C sliver on the scale is easy to miss the first time.

**Product / audience link:** the account's topic (science in the kitchen); recognized by home cooks who salt early. Evidence: Appendix B #1 (UIUC).
**Recognition:** thermometer as recurring element; 3-note motif (SFX) at hook, reveal, close; mustard tiles as signature.

**Style:** Swiss/Editorial grid with a Kinetic Typography accent on the reveal word, adapted to brand: flat fields only, one accent color (#E8B931), no gradients.
**Duration / format:** 25 s, 9:16, 30 fps.
**Effort:** S (GSAP, SVG shapes; no 3D).
**Asset needs:** exists: `assets/kuechenchemie-logo.png` (user-supplied, to be placed) / to create: pot, thermometer, tiles, bubbles (code-drawn SVG) / paid: none.
**Main risks:** the reveal sliver could look like an exaggerated graph (use the honest scale, no truncated axis); the thinking time must not feel like padding (keep 6 s max).

## Concept B: "Der ungeduldige Kochtopf"

**In one sentence:** An impatient pot "helps" with salt, the viewer recognizes the habit, and the pot's sudden calm is the physics answer.

**First seconds (0–3 s), concretely**
- Picture: a code-drawn pot (mustard) with a face in the center, water surface unmoved; a salt shaker enters from the right.
- Text: "Schneller kochen mit Salz?"
- Motion: the shaker pushes the pot sideways (cause and effect), `power3.out`, 0.4 s.
- Sound: a clack on impact.
- Hook mechanism: everyday relevance.

**The story** (4–6 beats)
1. 0–3 s — hook: shaker pushes the pot.
2. 3–9 s — escalation: pot taps its lid, looks at a timer, the bubbles arrive too slowly; each beat is a little more impatient.
3. 9–14 s — the salt is poured; the pot's face asks the viewer "Jetzt schneller?".
4. 14–20 s — reveal: the pot calms; text "Nein: Salz hebt den Siedepunkt nur um 0,5 °C an (bei 29,2 g pro kg Wasser)".
5. 20–23 s — visible change: the bubbles stop being the focus; the pot shrugs.
6. 23–25 s — close with the logo lockup.

**Open question:** "Jetzt schneller?" — answered at 14 s.
**Share trigger:** "that's so us" — the friend who always salts the water early gets tagged.
**Rewatch trigger:** the timer in the background shows the same time in every beat (visual gag).

**Product / audience link:** same evidence (Appendix B #1). Audience segment: home cooks who are impatient.
**Recognition:** the face of the pot as recurring element; motif SFX.

**Style:** Neo-Brutalism accent on Clay-like shapes is off-brand for the Senfgelb/Dunkelgrün palette; use flat Neo-Brutalism (thick borders) only with brand colors.
**Duration / format:** 25 s, 9:16.
**Effort:** M (character drawn in SVG, expressions).
**Asset needs:** create: pot with expressions (code-drawn SVG), shaker, timer / paid: none.
**Main risks:** the character can drift toward "mascot" without tying to the facts; a face expression set adds effort; the joke must not target the viewer.

## Concept C: "Die Skala"

**In one sentence:** A thermometer scale spans the whole frame; the viewer sees a sliver that is almost nothing, which is the honest answer to the myth.

**First seconds (0–3 s), concretely**
- Picture: full frame, dark green (#1F3D2B) with a vertical scale 0–100 °C; the top label reads "100 °C".
- Text: "Salz im Nudelwasser:/wie viel bringt das?"
- Motion: scale draws on from the bottom (SVG stroke, 0.6 s).
- Sound: low tone rising, then silence.
- Hook mechanism: visual surprise.

**The story** (4–6 beats)
1. 0–3 s — hook: the scale draws on.
2. 3–10 s — build-up: a mustard marker climbs the scale; text: "Ohne Salz: 100 °C".
3. 10–16 s — the marker moves on with salt: the marker's step is so small that it is hard to see; the frame zooms into the step.
4. 16–20 s — the reveal: "0,5 °C bei 29,2 g pro kg Wasser" with the honest unzoomed scale shown beside it.
5. 20–23 s — the consequence: the marker "waits" on the scale (deliberate stillness, 0.8 s).
6. 23–25 s — close with logo lockup.

**Open question:** "Wie viel bringt das?" — answered at 16 s.
**Share trigger:** "simple solution" — a fact that is short and checkable.
**Rewatch trigger:** the zoom reveals the step that was too small to see the first time.

**Product / audience link:** the same UIUC evidence. Audience: science-curious home cooks.
**Recognition:** the scale as recurring element; motif SFX on the marker's stop.

**Style:** Wireframe-like technical graphics (flat lines), accent #E8B931 on the marker only.
**Duration / format:** 25 s, 9:16.
**Effort:** S.
**Asset needs:** create: scale and marker (SVG) / paid: none.
**Main risks:** the zoom can look like exaggeration; the honest unzoomed scale must be shown in the same beat; the step is hard to see on mobile.

## C4. Recommendation

Concept A is recommended. Its question-and-reveal shape is the quiz format the user asked for, its thinking time is visible without a countdown, and its reveal lands on the one number the source supports. The trade-off: it has less personality than B, and the thinking time must stay short to avoid feeling like padding. Concept C is the bolder option (named risk: the zoom reveal can read as exaggeration).
